Quill

A calm reading tracker for GNOME.

Install
  • Install with GNOME Software Opens your app store; updates arrive like any other app

Quill is a reading tracker that lives on your computer, not on a website: shelves for what you've read, are reading and want to read, with covers and summaries found for you. No account, no followers, no feed.

Search Open Library or Google Books to add a book in seconds, or bring your library over from Openreads. Your shelves stay on your own machine.

How Quill compares

Quill is small on purpose. If one of these fits you better, it's a great choice:

AppMade forCompared with Quill
Goodreads Reviews, friends and recommendations on the web An online service that needs an account. Quill keeps your shelves on your own computer and has no social side.
The StoryGraph Reading stats and mood-based recommendations Also an online service with an account. Quill tracks dates, progress and ratings, without the charts.
Openreads Tracking your reading on your phone The closest in spirit, on Android and iOS. Quill imports its CSV export, so you can bring your library to the desktop.
Calibre Managing, converting and reading e-book files Organises the books themselves. Quill tracks what you read, on paper or screen, and never needs the files.

Questions

Where do the covers and summaries come from?

From Open Library, or Google Books if you switch the search source. They're fetched when you add a book, and you can also look for a different cover online or add a book entirely by hand.

Can I bring my library from another app?

Yes, from Openreads: import its CSV export and your shelves, ratings and reading dates come with it. Quill can also export your library back to a CSV at any time.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no sync service. Quill goes online only to search for books and download their covers and summaries.

Where is my library kept?

In Quill's own folder on your computer. Removing Quill with --delete-data removes it too, so export a CSV first if you want to keep it.

Why does GNOME Software list network and file permissions?

Quill uses the network to search Open Library and Google Books. It asks to read your system's files only so it can find your icon theme and draw its buttons with the same icons as the rest of your desktop. It never changes or uploads anything there.

Why isn't Quill on Flathub?

Flathub doesn't accept new apps made with AI assistance, and Quill was built with help from Claude. It's published from its own signed repository instead, and updates arrive through GNOME Software or flatpak update like any other app.

How do I install or remove it from a terminal?

To install:

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists quill \
    https://drvonmiau.github.io/Quill/index.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user quill io.github.drvonmiau.Quill

To remove Quill and its repository:

flatpak uninstall --user io.github.drvonmiau.Quill
flatpak remote-delete --user quill
